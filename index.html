Questions

    
V.
Before you book.

    

      
Is this a 360° spinning video booth?
No. 360°E is our name. Our booth is a full-length interactive magic mirror that takes photos and prints them on the spot.

      
Do guests get their photos right away?
Yes. Prints come out in about 30 seconds, and guests can scan a QR code to get their photos on their phone.

      
How many prints do we get?
Essential includes up to 200 prints, and extra prints are ₹30 each. Signature and Luxury include unlimited prints for your booked hours.

      
Can the print match our theme?
Yes. We design the layouts with your names, date and colours (3, 5 or 10 designs depending on your package), and you approve them before the event.

      
Who operates the booth?
Our host stays with the booth for your whole booking: 1 host in Essential and Signature, 2 in Luxury. We set up before guests arrive, keep the queue moving and pack up after.

      
Do you come outside Lucknow?
Lucknow is our base, and transport within Lucknow is included. For other cities, send us the venue on WhatsApp and we will tell you the travel cost.

      
How do I lock my date?
Send your details through the form above. We confirm the date on WhatsApp and share payment details. Your date is locked once the booking payment is received.
